## Evaluation goals

The project evaluated two tasks on Vakinha:

1. Find pet-related campaigns in Rio de Janeiro from the previous 15 days.
2. Choose the most recent campaign and simulate a R$ 20 donation.

The evaluation stopped before any actual donation. It examined the interface observed during the coursework. It does not describe the current state of the service.

## Methods

### Heuristic inspection

I inspected the task paths using Nielsen's ten usability heuristics. Each recorded violation included its location, evidence, severity, and a suggested correction.

The inspection recorded **21 violation occurrences**: **5 major**, **15 minor**, and **1 cosmetic**. These are occurrences across screens and paths, rather than 21 unique product defects. Repeated issues were grouped for prioritization.

### Usability tests with observation

The study included a pilot followed by two main sessions. Participants completed pre-test and post-test questionnaires and described their thoughts during the tasks.

The pilot informed changes to task presentation. Only the two main sessions were included in the quantitative analysis.

## Findings and recommendations

| Observed problem | Recommended change |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign cards did not clearly expose creation dates or result ordering. | Show creation dates and an explicit “Most recent” sort option. |
| Period filters could not express the requested date range. | Add a custom date range and keep the active filter visible. |
| Location options were difficult to find. | Make the complete list accessible and provide text search. |
| The R$ 25 minimum appeared after attempting a R$ 20 contribution. | Explain the minimum beside the amount field before submission. |
| The Pix modal combined copy confirmation, data collection, and promotional content. | Separate confirmation from optional registration and promotions. |

The observed sessions reinforced the inspection findings about recency, filtering, and donation rules.

## Results in context

Among the two main participants, average clarity of campaign publication dates was **1.5/5**. Confidence in selecting the most recent campaign averaged **2.0/5**.

Both participants encountered the R$ 25 minimum when attempting the R$ 20 task, which results in uncertainty and workarounds.

These ratings describe two sessions. They are descriptive findings, not population estimates.
